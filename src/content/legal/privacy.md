## Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser; similar technologies include local storage and pixels. We group them into three categories:

- **Essential:** needed for the site to work and to remember your cookie choice. These are always on.
- **Analytics:** help us understand which pages are useful and where the site can improve, using aggregated statistics.
- **Marketing:** help us measure our campaigns and show relevant ads on other platforms.

**How your choice works depends on where you are.** We use your approximate location (from your IP address, not stored) to show the right notice:

- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Quebec and Brazil:** analytics and marketing stay off until you choose **Accept**. If you choose **Reject**, only essential technologies run.
- **US states with consumer privacy laws** (California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia): a short notice appears and you can choose **Opt out** at any time.
- **Everywhere else:** no notice pops up, but you can opt out at any time.

You can change your mind whenever you like with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. We also treat a [Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) signal from your browser as a request to opt out.
