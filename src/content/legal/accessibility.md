## Our commitment

3 Horizons Interactive ("3 Horizons", "we", "us") is committed to making 3h-i.com usable by as many people as possible, including people with visual, hearing, motor, speech and cognitive disabilities. Accessibility is part of how we design, build and test every page, not a step at the end.

## The standard we work to

Our goal is for this website to conform to the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) at Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). We also track the newer WCAG 2.2 criteria and adopt them as we update the site.

WCAG 2.1 AA is the benchmark referenced by accessibility laws in the places we work, including:

- **Ontario, Canada:** the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and its Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation, which require WCAG 2.0 Level AA for public websites of covered organizations.
- **Canada:** the Accessible Canada Act and the federal Standard on Web Accessibility, and Quebec's standards for accessible websites (SGQRI 008 2.0).
- **United States:** the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), under which WCAG 2.1 AA is the widely used measure of an accessible website.
- **European Union:** the European Accessibility Act and EN 301 549, which build on WCAG 2.1 AA.

## Conformance status

We believe this website is **partially conformant** with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Partially conformant means most of the site meets the standard, and we know of a few areas, listed below, that do not fully meet it yet. We are working to close those gaps.

## What we have done

- **Keyboard access:** every link, button, menu and form can be reached and used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator.
- **Reduced motion:** the site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting. Smooth scrolling, parallax and the animated 3D artwork switch off or fall back to still images.
- **No WebGL needed:** the 3D graphics are decorative. Without WebGL they are replaced with static artwork, and nothing you need is lost.
- **Structure:** pages use real headings, landmarks and lists so screen readers can move through them quickly.
- **Text alternatives:** meaningful images have alt text; decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
- **Video:** the brand film on the About page plays muted, has no spoken content, and does not carry information that is not also in the page text.
- **Color and type:** we check text and controls against the WCAG contrast ratios and size text in relative units, so pages work when you zoom to 200%.
- **Forms:** every contact form field has a visible label, and required fields and errors are announced.

## Known limitations

- **Text over imagery:** some headlines sit on photos, gradients or animated artwork, and contrast can drop in a few places as the background moves. We are reviewing each of these.
- **AI visibility scan:** the results panel animates its score and steps. Screen readers receive the final result, but the progress steps may not all be announced.
- **Older articles and case studies:** a few older images may have alt text that is brief or missing. We are reviewing them as we update each page.
- **Third party content:** links to LinkedIn, Instagram and other services take you to sites we do not control, which may not meet the same standard.

If you find a barrier that is not listed here, please tell us so we can fix it.

## How we test

We review the site with automated checkers (such as axe and Lighthouse) and with manual testing: keyboard only navigation, zoom and reflow, contrast checks, and screen readers including VoiceOver on macOS and iOS and NVDA on Windows. ## Compatibility

The site is designed to work with recent versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge on desktop and mobile, and with the screen readers listed above. Very old browsers may not display every feature, but the content stays readable.
