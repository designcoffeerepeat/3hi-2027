## Where AI helps first

Real estate teams spend hours on tasks that follow a pattern: answering first inquiries, writing listing descriptions and pulling market comparisons. These are good first candidates for AI.

## Practical uses

1. **Lead follow-up.** An assistant answers new inquiries in minutes and books calls with agents.
2. **Listing copy.** Draft descriptions from property details, then edit for voice and accuracy.
3. **Market snapshots.** Summarize recent sales and trends for a neighborhood in seconds.
4. **Document review.** Flag missing items in contracts and disclosures before they slow a deal.

> AI should handle the busywork so agents can spend more time with clients.

## Start small

Pick one workflow, measure the hours it takes today and run a pilot for a month. Expand once the numbers show it pays back.
