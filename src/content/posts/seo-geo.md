## Search now has two front doors

For twenty years, getting found meant ranking on page one of Google. That still matters. But a growing share of buyers now ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity first, and they often act on the answer without ever clicking a link.

That does not make SEO obsolete. It means SEO is now one of two channels you have to manage: the classic results page, and the AI answer that sits above it or replaces it entirely.

> If an AI model cannot understand who you are and why you are credible, it will recommend someone it can.

## What GEO actually means

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of making your brand easy for AI models to find, understand and cite. It builds on the same foundations as SEO: clear structure, strong authority and content that answers real questions.

![Woman in a varsity jacket](/images/blog-jacket.webp)

## Five moves to make this quarter

1. **Fix the technical basics.** Fast pages, clean indexing and structured data help both crawlers and models.
2. **Answer real questions.** Write pages that answer what buyers actually ask, in plain language.
3. **Build citations, not just links.** Mentions on trusted industry sites shape how models describe you.
4. **Keep facts consistent.** Your name, services and locations should match everywhere they appear.
5. **Track AI answers.** Check how assistants describe you each month, then close the gaps.
