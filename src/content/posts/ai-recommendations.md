## Shortlists start in a chat window

A buyer asks an assistant for the best agency, store or tool in your category. In seconds they get three or four names. If yours is not one of them, you never get the visit, the call or the chance to compete.

## What models look for

Assistants favor brands they can read easily and confirm elsewhere. That means pages open to AI crawlers, clear statements of who you are and what you sell, and consistent facts across your site, profiles and listings.

> A model can only recommend what it can understand. Make understanding you easy.

## Where to start

1. **Open the door.** Make sure your robots rules do not block the major AI crawlers.
2. **Say it plainly.** Put a clear one paragraph summary of your business on your home and About pages.
3. **Earn mentions.** Reviews, press and partner pages give models outside proof.
4. **Check the answers.** Ask the main assistants about your category each month and note what changes.

## Keep it going

AI visibility is not a one-time fix. New content, new reviews and new competitors change the answers, so treat it like SEO: a habit, measured over time.
