## Growth is a system

Instead of one big campaign, growth marketing runs a steady stream of small experiments across the funnel and keeps what works.

## Build the loop

1. **Find the leak.** Use analytics to see where most visitors drop off.
2. **Form a hypothesis.** Write down what you will change and what you expect to happen.
3. **Test it.** Run an A/B test long enough to reach a clear result.
4. **Keep or kill.** Roll out winners, document losers and move on.

> The fastest growing brands are not the ones with the best ideas. They are the ones that test the most ideas.

## Do not forget retention

Email and loyalty programs that bring customers back often lift profit faster than new acquisition. Measure lifetime value, not only first orders.
