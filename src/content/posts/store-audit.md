## Start where the traffic is

Before redesigning anything, open your analytics and list the ten pages with the most visits. Most of the gains in this audit come from fixing those pages first.

## The ten point checklist

1. **Speed.** Test your home, collection and product pages on mobile. Aim for under two seconds.
2. **Apps.** Remove apps you no longer use. Each one can add scripts to every page.
3. **Navigation.** Can a new visitor find your best seller in two taps?
4. **Search.** Try five searches a customer would make. Do the results make sense?
5. **Product photos.** Show scale, detail and the product in use.
6. **Product copy.** Lead with the benefit, then the details.
7. **Reviews.** Put them near the add to cart button, not at the bottom of the page.
8. **Shipping and returns.** State them clearly before checkout.
9. **Checkout.** Turn on express payment options and remove unneeded fields.
10. **Structured data.** Make sure products show price, stock and reviews to search engines and AI assistants.

> Most stores do not need a redesign. They need ten small fixes done well.

## What to do next

Pick the three items that affect the most visitors, fix them this month and measure the change before moving on.
