## Foundations still win

Every few months a new ranking factor gets attention. Meanwhile, the sites that grow steadily tend to get the basics right: pages that can be crawled, indexed and loaded quickly.

## Where to focus

1. **Crawl and index.** Check that important pages are indexed and that thin or duplicate pages are not.
2. **Mobile speed.** Measure your main templates on a mid-range phone, not only on desktop.
3. **Internal links.** Link from strong pages to the pages you want to rank.
4. **Structured data.** Mark up products, articles, organizations and FAQs.
5. **Rendering.** Make sure key content is visible without waiting on scripts.

> If a crawler cannot see it, it cannot rank it, and an AI model cannot cite it.

## Measure what changes

Track indexed pages, Core Web Vitals and rankings for a fixed keyword set. Review them monthly so you can tie each fix to a result.
