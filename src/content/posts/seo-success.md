## Focus beats volume

The programs that grow fastest start with a short list of high-intent keywords, the searches people make when they are ready to buy or book, and map each one to a single page.

## Content that answers the search

For each target keyword, look at what already ranks and ask what is missing. Then build the page that answers the question better: clearer, more complete and easier to scan.

> Rankings follow usefulness. Write the page your buyer wishes existed.

## Links with relevance

A handful of links from respected industry sites will do more than dozens from unrelated ones. Partnerships, original data and expert commentary are reliable ways to earn them.

## Review and repeat

Check rankings and conversions monthly, refresh pages that slip and expand into new keywords once the first set is performing.
